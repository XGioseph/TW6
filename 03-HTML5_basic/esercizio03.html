Giuseppe Colucci is an Italian computer science student currently attending the third year at the Department of Computer Science of the University of Naples “Parthenope.” Passionate about technology, programming, and videogames, he combines curiosity and creativity in exploring how software and innovation can shape new digital experiences.

His academic interests include software development, artificial intelligence, and computer graphics, with a particular enthusiasm for understanding how complex systems work behind modern interactive applications and games. Colucci is constantly improving his technical skills, participating in university projects and personal experiments aimed at bridging theory and practice.

Beyond his studies, he follows the evolution of the gaming industry and the open-source community, seeing in them a dynamic field for creativity, collaboration, and continuous learning. He aspires to contribute to the development of intelligent and engaging technologies that connect people and ideas.

Curious and motivated, Giuseppe Colucci represents a new generation of computer scientists inspired by innovation, guided by passion, and driven by the desire to transform imagination into technology.
